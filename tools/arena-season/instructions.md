# Drawing instructions

You are given one Task: a circuit's name and its SPICE netlist. There is no
figure. Draw the circuit as a schematic in Analog Canvas.

- Draw exactly the devices and connections of the netlist in one Cell. Keep
  each device's type, model and parameters. You may rename nets and
  instances; you may not change what connects to what.
- Draw it the way you would present it to a reader who knows circuits.
- Save the result as an Analog Canvas Project file named after the Task id,
  for example `T001.icproj.json`.
- One attempt per Task, at most 15 minutes, with no human edits.
